PSARA compliance

The Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005 governs who may lawfully supply private security personnel in India. 20fourr verifies each provider's PSARA licence before listing them, and re-checks expiry on a rolling basis.

When a licence lapses, the provider is blocked from search and from accepting new bookings automatically — not at an admin's discretion — until the renewed licence is verified.

Armed duty is held to a second standard

A PSARA licence permits an agency to supply personnel. It does not permit anyone to carry a firearm. Armed bookings therefore require a valid individual weapon licence, verified independently of the agency licence.

  • The weapon licence is checked per officer, not per agency
  • An officer without a current, verified licence cannot be assigned to an armed booking at all
  • If the licence lapses mid-contract, the officer is withdrawn from armed duty until it is renewed

Your data

Provider identity documents are never exposed to clients. Contact details are masked until you have a confirmed, paid booking with that provider. We process personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

  • Booking details are shared only with the agency performing the job
  • Licence and ID documents are held for verification and are not shared with other users
  • You can request access to or deletion of your data through our privacy contact

What verification does not mean

Being straight about the limits is part of being trustworthy. Verification is a check on documents and history — it is not a guarantee of future conduct, and no platform can honestly claim otherwise.

Read this before you book

20fourr is a technology platform. The security services themselves are performed by independent, PSARA-licensed agencies and their personnel, and responsibility for their conduct on duty sits with them.

We are not an emergency service. In an emergency, contact the police on 112 first, then raise an incident on your booking so the record, the agency and our compliance team stay aligned.

A verified badge confirms a document was checked on a date. It does not predict behaviour. Ratings, check-in records and the incident process exist precisely because paperwork alone is not enough.

Report a concern

If something is wrong — an officer who did not report, conduct you are unhappy with, or a document you believe is not genuine — raise it against the booking. It reaches our compliance team and the agency at the same time, with the booking record attached.

  • Falsified documents result in immediate and permanent removal from the platform
  • Repeated no-shows or persistently poor ratings lead to delisting
  • Check-in and check-out are recorded against the shift you paid for, so attendance is evidence rather than opinion

Getting started

6 questions
Do I need an account to see prices?

No. Pricing is visible to anyone. You only create an account at the point of booking, because we need somewhere to send your confirmation and receipt.

Is 20fourr a security agency?

No. 20fourr is a technology platform that connects clients with independent, PSARA-licensed security agencies and personnel. We do not employ guards or provide security services ourselves, and we are not a private security agency under the Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005.

Which cities do you cover?

Coverage expands as verified agencies join. Enter your city on the search page to see live availability — if we have no verified provider there yet, you will be told so rather than shown an unverified one.

How quickly can I get someone on site?

For most roles in covered cities, same-day and next-day bookings are possible subject to availability. Armed personnel and personal security officers usually need more notice because licence and assignment checks are stricter.

Can individuals book, or is this only for businesses?

Both. A homeowner booking one guard for a wedding and a company booking forty for a warehouse use the same platform. Larger and recurring requirements are quoted separately.

Is there a minimum booking?

Minimums are set by each agency and shown on the provider's listing before you book, along with the shift lengths they accept.

Verification and safety

8 questions
What does PSARA-verified actually mean?

It means an admin has seen the agency's licence issued under the Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005 and confirmed it is current. It is not a claim the agency makes about itself — a provider cannot award themselves a badge.

Who does the verification?

Our compliance team, before a provider is ever listed. Documents are reviewed by a person, not auto-approved by an upload form.

What is checked for an individual officer?

Identity documents, police verification where applicable, and — for armed roles — a separately verified weapon licence. Which checks a provider has cleared is shown on their listing.

What does the Background check badge cover?

It means police verification is on file for that provider and has been read by an admin. It does not guarantee future conduct; it confirms the paperwork exists and was reviewed.

How is the Top rated badge earned?

It requires an average of 4.5 or higher across at least five completed bookings. It cannot be bought, and it is removed automatically if the average drops.

What happens if a licence expires mid-contract?

Listings are tied to licence validity. If a licence lapses, the provider stops appearing in search and cannot accept new bookings until it is renewed and re-verified.

Can I see the actual documents?

No. Licence and identity documents contain personal data and are not shared with clients. What you see is the verification status our team assigned after reviewing them.

How do you handle my personal data?

Data is processed under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Booking details are shared with the assigned agency because they need them to perform the job, and not with anyone else.

Roles and services

7 questions
What is the difference between a security guard and a bouncer?

A security guard covers static duty at premises — offices, warehouses, sites and residential societies. A bouncer handles door control and crowd management at events and venues, which is a more physical, public-facing role.

What is the difference between an armed gunman and a security guard?

An armed gunman carries a firearm and therefore needs a separately verified weapon licence on top of PSARA registration. We check that licence independently, and no armed booking is accepted without it.

What does a personal security officer do?

Close protection for an individual, family or executive — travel, site visits, events and daily movement — rather than guarding a fixed location.

Can I request female security personnel?

Yes, where available. Many venues, events and residential requirements need female guards, and it is a filter on the search page.

Can I request ex-army or ex-police personnel?

Yes. Where a provider lists prior service, it is shown on their profile. Rates for these personnel are typically higher.

Do guards bring their own uniform and equipment?

Uniform is standard and provided by the agency. Additional equipment — metal detectors, radios, vehicles — varies by provider and is listed where offered.

Can I book the same guard again?

Yes, subject to their availability. Repeat bookings with a provider you have used before can be made directly from your booking history.

Pricing, GST and payments

7 questions
Is GST included in the price I see?

Yes. The figure on screen is the amount payable, broken into base amount, platform fee and GST so you can see exactly what you are paying for.

Why do rates differ between providers?

Rates reflect the role, the city, shift length, experience, whether the officer is armed, and the agency's own pricing. You see each provider's rate before choosing, rather than one blended number.

Are there hidden charges?

No. The breakdown shown before you commit is the full amount. Any additional cost — extended hours, overtime, extra personnel added later — is quoted and approved separately.

How do I pay?

Payments are made online through our payment partner at the time of booking. Card, UPI and netbanking are supported.

Do I get a GST invoice?

Yes. A tax invoice is issued for every booking and is available from your booking history, so businesses can claim input credit where eligible.

Do I pay the guard directly?

No. Paying personnel directly is outside the platform and voids the protections that come with a tracked booking. All payment runs through 20fourr to the agency.

Do you offer contracts or monthly billing?

Yes, for recurring requirements such as warehouses, societies and offices. Use the bulk quote option rather than booking shift by shift.

Booking, changes and cancellations

5 questions
What do I get when a booking is confirmed?

Written confirmation with the assigned officer's name, an ID reference, reporting time and site address. The agency receives the same record, so nothing depends on a phone call being remembered.

Can I change the dates or hours after booking?

Changes can be requested from your booking. Whether they are accepted depends on the provider's availability, and any price difference is shown before you confirm.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation terms and any applicable charges are shown on the provider's listing before you book and again at checkout. Terms tighten closer to the reporting time, because the agency has already allocated personnel.

Can the agency cancel on me?

It is rare and it counts against their rating. If it happens, our team works to place a replacement from another verified provider, and you are refunded if we cannot.

Can I book for multiple locations at once?

Yes, though each site is treated as a separate deployment so that check-in can be tracked correctly against the right address.

During the booking

6 questions
How do I know the officer actually arrived?

Check-in and check-out are recorded against the shift you paid for and visible on your booking, so attendance is a matter of record rather than a claim.

What if I have a problem while the officer is on duty?

Raise an issue against the live booking. It goes to our team and the agency at the same time, with the booking record attached.

What happens in a serious incident?

Contact emergency services first — 20fourr is not an emergency service. Then raise an incident on the booking so the agency, our compliance team and the record are all aligned for anything that follows.

Who is liable if something goes wrong?

The security services are performed by the independent, PSARA-licensed agency you booked, and liability for their conduct sits with them. Our role is verification, booking and record-keeping. The full position is set out in our Terms of service.

Can I extend a shift while it is running?

Yes, subject to the officer's availability. The extension is priced and confirmed on the booking so it stays part of the same record.

How do ratings work?

You rate the provider after the booking closes. Ratings drive the Top rated badge and search position, and persistently poor ratings lead to removal from the platform.

For security agencies

6 questions
How do I list my agency on 20fourr?

Apply through the provider sign-up. You will be asked for your PSARA licence, company registration, GST details and personnel records before your listing can go live.

What does it cost to list?

Listing is free. 20fourr charges a platform fee on completed bookings, which is shown transparently to the client as part of the price breakdown.
